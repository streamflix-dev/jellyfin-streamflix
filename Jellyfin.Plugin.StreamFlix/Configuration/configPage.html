Profiles

Each profile is linked to its own Jellyfin user, so watch history, Continue Watching and favorites stay separate. Add a PIN or lock a profile to a Google account from Manage profiles.
The Owner profile is linked to an administrator account but has no PIN or Google lock. Anyone signed in to your household can open it — consider protecting it.
